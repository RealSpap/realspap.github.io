I took 20 of my own registry entries. Two separate reviews re-read 100 of their statements at the source. The second review could read 49 statements, and it found 10 of them wrong or saying more than their source. So the two reviews did not apply the same strictness. 3 statements could not be read at their source. They are not counted.
