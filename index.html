391 Protocol entries

Multisig overlap

391 protocol entries, 729 confirmed multisig contracts, 25 chains, same method throughout. The widest single signer key sits on 5 independent protocols at once, and one Safe governs the core L1 contracts of 5 Superchain chains. On Ethereum mainnet alone, 8 signers hold keys on 2 or more independent protocols; 7 of them are named in the protocols' own public sources. Dozens of protocols reuse the same or an overlapping signer set across multiple chains.

23 Cases

DeFi admin-key risk

A single key (a bare EOA or a 1-of-N Safe) still holding an admin role that can mint, pause, or redirect funds: the pattern behind Wasabi Protocol's $5.9M loss in April 2026. 99 DeFi protocols and Morpho vault owners checked by hand on-chain; 23 single-key admin cases (2 Critical, 6 High, 7 Medium, 8 Low). Several are bounded by a timelock or a guardian, and each case says so.
