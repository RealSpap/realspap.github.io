570 Safes

Multisig overlap

Checked at three scales, same method every time: 343 protocols, 570 confirmed Safe contracts across 25 chains, from Ethereum mainnet to Optimism's Superchain to Arbitrum and 10 more L2s and sidechains. 8 named individuals hold signer keys across 2+ unrelated protocols on mainnet alone, and 30+ protocols reuse the identical signer set on every chain they deploy to. The live version replays the actual on-chain event history (AddedOwner, RemovedOwner, SafeSetup) to recompute current ownership fresh on every run, not a point-in-time snapshot.

DeFi upgrade-key risk

Where a single externally-owned account, not a multisig, still holds an admin role able to mint, pause, or redirect funds in a live protocol. Well over 70 protocols checked by hand; 12 cases came back genuinely live and at risk, anchored on Wasabi Protocol's real ~$5.9M loss to a compromised deployer key.

42 Incidents

Exploit postmortems

Reconstructing what happened on-chain after a protocol gets hit, transaction by transaction, from the transfers and calls themselves rather than the announcement thread. One example: DefiLlama tracked WealthManagementV2 as a $26,414 "private key compromise." It was never compromised, the proxy was owned by the attacker's own wallet from deployment, and the real drain was $422,251, 16x DefiLlama's figure. 34 such corrections across 51 incidents, about $798.5M recomputed from primary sources.
