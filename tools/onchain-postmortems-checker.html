Onchain postmortems

How did 72 on-chain exploits actually happen?

At least $954.3M lost across 72 incidents, all but one reconstructed from raw chain data (Tectonic on Cronos, whose chain was rolled back past the attack). $617.4M of it is independently recomputed; the rest, marked †, is sourced from the protocol, press or DefiLlama.

Data checked against the repository on 30 October 2026 (commit 432eb21): 72 incidents, the latest dated 2026-10-14.

        Check an address against this dataset
        
  
        Paste a 0x address. Checks it only against addresses that each incident's writeup ties to the attack itself: the attacker, its attack contracts and the address its funds were sent to. An address the writeup only suspects, or that the press named, is left out. It is not a general wallet safety check, and "no match" says nothing beyond that.

        Method
        Each entry rebuilds one incident from raw chain data with a script in the repository, and the writeup says what it could and could not establish. This page copies the repository’s index, row for row, and adds nothing.
        
    
        Dates
        Dataset read on 30 October 2026 from commit 432eb21 (data/incidents.json). Latest incident dated 2026-10-14. The page itself makes no network request.
        
    
        Limits
        13 of 72 loss figures are sourced from the protocol, the press or DefiLlama (†) and not recomputed. Only 46 incidents have a full 0x address that the writeup ties to the attack (113 addresses in all, reviewed by hand on 30 October 2026). The others cannot be matched by address here: the chain does not use 0x addresses, the address is truncated, only suspected or named by the press, or none is given.
