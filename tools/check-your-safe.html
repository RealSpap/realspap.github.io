Check your Safe

A small public utility from the multisig-overlap-showcase research project. It reads a Safe's owner list directly from chain, in your browser, and checks each owner against the identities already named in that project's public README.

Works on a Gnosis Safe. Any other contract, or a plain wallet address, will just fail the check below, that is expected and not an error in this tool.

This free check only looks at three addresses, the ones already named in the public README for this research. The licensed method behind this project cross-references 343 protocols and 570 Safes. If that fuller check is relevant to your organization, ask about it at x.com/RealSpap.

This page calls public Ethereum RPC endpoints directly from your browser to read the Safe's owner list. Nothing you enter is sent to any server operated by this project. Source and methodology: github.com/RealSpap/multisig-overlap-showcase.
