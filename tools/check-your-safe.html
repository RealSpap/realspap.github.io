Check your Safe

A small public utility from the multisig-overlap-showcase research project. It reads a Safe's owner list directly from chain, in your browser, and checks each owner against the signers already documented in that project's public README.

Reads Ethereum mainnet only. A Safe deployed only on Base, Optimism, Arbitrum or another chain cannot be read here, and a Safe at the same address on another chain may have different owners. Any other contract, or a plain wallet address, will just fail the check below.

This free check only looks at three signer addresses documented in this research. The licensed method behind this project cross-references 391 protocol entries and 729 multisig addresses. If that fuller check is relevant to your organization, ask about it at x.com/RealSpap.

This page reads the Safe's owner list from Ethereum mainnet directly in your browser. When you press Check, the address is sent only to these public RPC endpoints, in this order, the next one only if the previous one fails: ethereum-rpc.publicnode.com, eth.drpc.org, 1rpc.io. The page loads no third-party fonts, scripts or trackers. Source and methodology: github.com/RealSpap/multisig-overlap-showcase.
