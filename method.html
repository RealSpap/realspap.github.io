Method

Method and limits.

What I read, what I do not read, how to run it again, and where it stops.

What I read

Each entry of the registry rebuilds one incident from raw chain data (eth_getLogs, decoded transaction receipts, eth_call reads), not from press summaries.

Each folder keeps its Python script, its registre_hypotheses.csv (every claim broken into a hypothesis with a test that could prove it wrong) and the raw output of the script in resultats_*.txt. A figure I recompute can be checked against the file that produced it.

Role claims (admin, multisig, governance, infrastructure) come only from on-chain records and cited public sources. They are not allegations of wrongdoing, fraud or illegal conduct.

What I do not read

71 of the 72 entries rebuild their incident from public chain data, and their script reads the chain live. The exception is tectonic-cronos: according to Cronos’s report, the chain was rolled back past that attack, so the attack cannot be rebuilt from the chain.

18 entries give a figure that is a known floor, not a full total. The README of the repository lists each one with the reason.

13 figures are marked †. They come from the protocol’s own post-mortem, from the press or from DefiLlama, and I did not recompute them. The footnote of each names the source.

Funds that the attacker returned, or that were reversed on-chain, are deducted from the loss. Reimbursements that a protocol paid from its own reserves are not.

How to run it again

pip install web3
python3 sandbox-oft-delegate-hijack/reconstruct_exploit.py

The historical figures should match sandbox-oft-delegate-hijack/resultats_reconstruction_2026-09-09.txt. Balances and roles that the script reads today may have moved since.

To check that the totals of the README add up from the Index, run python3 check_readme_consistency.py.

Limits

This is independent research, not a security audit. I am not affiliated with any protocol, auditor or outlet cited in a folder.

A finding is a snapshot. The state of a chain changes after publication, and the reports are not updated by themselves.

No protocol or team discussed here commissioned or audited this work. It is not legal, financial or investment advice.
