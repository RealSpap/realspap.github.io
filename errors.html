Find my errors

Find my errors.

If a figure, a source, an attribution or a sentence on this site or in onchain-postmortems is wrong, or says more than its source, tell me.

Write to me

contact@realspap.com, with onchain-postmortems in the subject. Use this for anything that should not be public: a fix still in progress, a secret committed by mistake, a correction you would rather not discuss in the open.

Or open a public issue

Open an issue on the repository. It is public, with the name of your GitHub account. Put no e-mail address in it.

What to include

The place concerned, the statement you dispute, and what supports your reading, so that anyone can read it again: a transaction hash, a block number, an address with the call to make, a line of code or a public document.

What is in scope

  • A statement, a figure, a date or an attribution in an entry, in the Index or in the corrections table.
  • A script that queries the chain and returns something different from its saved output for a historical block.
  • A dollar figure that does not follow from its source files.
  • Anything that identifies a person who should not be identified, or a secret committed by mistake.

Out of scope: the contracts and systems of the projects named here. If you found a flaw in one of them, report it to that team through its own channel.

What to expect

I read reports as time allows. I promise no delay. When a statement is shown to be wrong, I correct it in the entry itself and record the correction in CHANGELOG.md. If a report concerns an issue that is not fixed yet, I keep the detail out of the repository until the team concerned has had time to fix it. I give credit on request. There is no reward in money or tokens.

The rules are in SECURITY.md.

What has been corrected so far

The corrections log has 38 rows on 36 entries: 26 corrections, 9 reconciliations and 3 discoveries. 3 of the rows correct my own earlier text. The other 35 concern figures, labels, dates or classifications published by the press, by DefiLlama or by a protocol.
