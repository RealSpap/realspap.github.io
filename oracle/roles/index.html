The holder of a role is not always the one who executes it: one measured case

A contract page shows who holds the admin power. We checked whether the holder shown is the one the contract actually lets act, by simulating each privileged call from each holder. On what we measured (46 call forms probed on 14 AccessControl contracts (Ethereum 4, Robinhood Chain 7, Plasma 1, HyperEVM 2), three contracts measured by hand (a beacon and its tokens on Robinhood Chain, Ethena's minting contract, Aave V3's pool configurator) and 26 Ownable owners on Ethereum) the answer was the same every time: the holder shown executes what the role is named for, 0 divergences. One contract still showed a power the three-part score does not carry.

How much was measured

Of 171 EVM targets classified, AccessControl is rare: 14 contracts could be probed, with 46 call forms in all, 0 divergences. Blocks: Ethereum 26,173,603; Robinhood Chain 86,351,252; Plasma 34,834,451; HyperEVM latest, about 48,320,159 (its public RPC ignores the block number); two public nodes that agreed, except Plasma, Monad, Tempo and HyperEVM (one node).

8 of the 46 forms had no executor among the holders (3 were answered by a business guard; the other 5 are not read either (a plausible argument would be needed to call them). None of the 8 is counted as a divergence). Solana (26 targets) was not part of this pass.

Reserve. The candidates probed are the holders read from each contract. An executor outside the holders (a timelock, another route) is visible only through a measurement by hand, as for the three contracts above.

The case: Ethena's USDe minting contract, Ethereum mainnet

0xe3490297a08d6fC8Da46Edb7B6142E4F461b62D3. Four accounts without code, holding GATEKEEPER_ROLE, can switch off minting and redeeming and remove any minter or redeemer, with no delay. Read at block 26,173,362 on two public nodes (ethereum-rpc.publicnode.com, gateway.tenderly.co/public/mainnet) that agreed. The accounts, shortened: 0x0F56…5BD9, 0x4960…8996, 0xAB91…A7dC, 0xb6EC…CCA3.

These four accounts are not part of the (admin key, multisig, timelock) score, which is why this page exists: a power worth stating next to the score.

The log range may need narrowing on some nodes (use several requests of two million blocks). The simulation changes nothing on chain.

What this does not show

  • The twenty minter and redeemer accounts act through signed orders, which a plain eth_call does not exercise: not measured.
  • The collateral-manager Safe's own functions were not found among the selectors this run could name (about half of the 57 selectors in the code are unnamed): its power over collateral is not measured.
  • One contract out of 197 tracked. A simulation shows who is allowed to call, not what the call would do to funds.
  • Source of the measurement: the project's own run of 12 October 2026 (two nodes, one pinned block). Replayed independently the same day with a script that shares no code with it: the four holders rebuilt from the contract's RoleGranted and RoleRevoked events, each without code, each simulated from on both nodes, a stranger refused.
