Why this score

A 6-of-13 legacy multisig with no delay upgrades the program, and a council of 5 (3 votes pass) can change every top-level authority. Counting that council path as full-power is a disclosed judgment call.

judgment call: admin path counted as full-power

The upgrade authority is a 2021-era multisig forked from the archived Serum template, 6-of-13, with no timelock field in its code. State.admin_authority is the treasury of a council-only governance record (community voting is disabled): 5 members with 1 vote each and a 50% threshold. It can reassign the other authorities and so choose validators, and the cap on stake moved per epoch has no enforced ceiling. No instruction sends user principal to an admin address. One key sits on both the upgrade multisig and the council, so one compromise reaches both paths. The score moved down from the scouted upper bound. DefiLlama listed over $200M TVL.
