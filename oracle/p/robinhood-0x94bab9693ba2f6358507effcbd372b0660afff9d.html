Why this score

A real 3-of-5 Safe behind a real 21-day upgrade delay - undercut by a single bare-EOA override that can zero the delay on demand.

UpgradeGatekeeper enforces a genuine 21-day notice period (1,814,400s) before the 3-of-5 "master" Safe’s upgrade takes effect. But the gatekeeper’s own verified source exposes cutUpgradeNoticePeriod(), callable by a single securityCouncilAddress - confirmed on-chain as a bare EOA, not the "3-of-5 security council" third-party write-ups (L2BEAT, a Medium post) describe. One private key can zero the 21-day window the instant the Safe queues an upgrade.
