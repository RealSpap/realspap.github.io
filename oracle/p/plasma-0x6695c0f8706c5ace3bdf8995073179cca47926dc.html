Why this score

yzUSD, syzUSD and yzPP are proxies owned by a 2-day timelock (4-of-5 Safe), each token’s ADMIN_ROLE sits with a second timelock on a 12-hour delay (3-of-5 Safe, the same five bare-EOA signers), and that 3-of-5 Safe also holds an instant, uncapped pool-valuation seat on the yzPP first-loss pool with no delay in front of it.

Traced live on 2026-09-20 on two RPCs and adversarially re-verified, pushed on-chain the same day (Plasma testnet block 34083939). The old adminKey 20 was a placeholder built on a false negative: it tested the OpenZeppelin-4 TIMELOCK_ADMIN_ROLE hash on OpenZeppelin-5 timelocks, which administer through DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE, and both timelocks are in fact their own only admin. Only the 2-day timelock (owner of every ProxyAdmin) can upgrade the logic. Through the 12-hour timelock the same keys can setTreasury (deposits are forwarded straight to a bare-EOA treasury), move fee and cap levers, and by granting roles mark NAV down and mint against nothing. The weakest path is the Safe’s direct POOL_MANAGER and DISTRIBUTOR seat on yzPP: updatePool can write the pool’s value in one batch, and four of the five signers use it weekly. Neither timelock has a bypass, but their only cancellers are the proposer Safe and one of its own signers, so the delay is a visibility window, not a veto. Scored 65/55/0 by the instant-Safe-seat convention (Radiant, Monad Aave). Pause seats, bounded EOA powers and the bare-EOA custody of the roughly $56M reserve are disclosed, not scored. The same five keys also own Safes on Ethereum and Monad, where Yuzu is not tracked. Full trace: data/finding_2026-09-20-yuzu-authority-trace.md.
