Why this score

A HYPE staking vault, about $41.7M delegated at the 2026-09-17 read. Every fund-moving function sits behind MANAGER_ROLE, held by a proxy with an unverified implementation, so the score treats it as the binding constraint.

unverified MANAGER_ROLE holder binds the score

That is 500,801 HYPE delegated to validators. Access control is deferred to an external RoleRegistry: its owner, a 1-of-1 Safe, can upgrade the vault and grant roles, and OPERATOR_ROLE is a separate 1-of-3 Safe that counts the owner Safe among its signers. MANAGER_ROLE gates deposit, stake, unstake, spotSend and the other fund-moving calls, and its holder's implementation was deliberately not reverse-engineered. If it is itself gated by the RoleRegistry owner, real risk may be lower; not assumed either way. Ventuals has the same design but verified source, and scores much higher. No dex-admin path was found.
