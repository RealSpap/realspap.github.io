Why this score

undelayed price-source path

Seven authority paths exist and four feed a score. Program upgrade is a 5-of-10 Squads v4 multisig with a 24h delay, the market owner is 4-of-10 with 12h, the Scope price aggregator admin is 4-of-10 with none, and Scope's own upgrade is 5-of-10 with 24h. The upgrade, Scope admin and Scope upgrade multisigs use the same 10 keys, and the market owner shares 9 of them. Klend rejects prices more than 1,000 bps (SOL) or 300 bps (USDC) from the TWAP, but the same admin can re-map the TWAP source, so no credit was given. Scoring the providers behind Scope is still open, and the emergency council (restrict-only) has an unresolved controller.

  • ✗ Bypass paths of the delay checked and ruled out
