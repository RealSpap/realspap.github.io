Why this score

In April 2026 a 2-of-5 multisig with no delay, which held Drift's admin key, was used to reassign it to an attacker (press reports $285M drained). The upgrade multisig is now 4-of-7 with a 1-hour delay, still short of strong.

retroactive backtest of the 2026-04-01 exploit

The repo verified on chain the two transactions on 2026-04-01 that used the old multisig to reassign Drift's admin key. The roughly 31 withdrawals were not re-derived. That multisig also had a non-default config_authority, not traced further. Retroactive score of that setup: 32. Now the upgrade multisig is 4-of-7 (6 can vote), 1-hour delay, autonomous. The DAO realm authority is self-governed: token vote with a 2% yes threshold, 1-day hold-up, 4-day voting, plus a 5-seat council that can only veto. The oracle sub-score rests on one Switchboard-fed market (AI16Z), not every market.

3 of 6 requirements met

  • ✓ Admin power is not a bare key
  • ✓ At least a 3-of-5 multisig
  • ✗ A confirmed delay before changes apply, no bypass
  • ✗ Bypass paths of the delay checked and ruled out
  • ✗ Price feed not under one hand
  • ✓ No key shared with another tracked protocol

Exit window: a delay with a bypass, or one not ruled out, the time a user has to leave after a harmful change is queued.
