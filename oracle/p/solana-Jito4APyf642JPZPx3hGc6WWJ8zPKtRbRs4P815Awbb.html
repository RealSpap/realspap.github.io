Why this score

New pool code could move all stake, and that path is the SPL Stake Pool upgrade committee (6 of 10, no delay). The pool's own roles are bounded: fee changes are delayed or capped, and no human key can mint JitoSOL.

inherits the stake pool committee

The pool manager is the native treasury of the Jito DAO governance (council threshold 51% of a council mint of 7 tokens, 5-day vote, 2-day cool-off, no hold-up); its fee changes take effect two epochs later or are capped, and WithdrawStake stays permissionless. The staker is a Steward program account and the stake deposit authority an Interceptor program account. The mint authority is the pool's withdraw PDA. The scorer re-reads these roles on every run and skips the target if one changed. DefiLlama listed over $900M TVL on 2026-09-17.
