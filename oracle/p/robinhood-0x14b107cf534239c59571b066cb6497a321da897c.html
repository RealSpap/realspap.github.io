Why this score

Deposit and withdrawal custody vault for Arcus Perps. Its admin role is held by a real 24-hour timelock and, in parallel, by a validator contract whose 2-of-3 bare-EOA quorum can, per Arcus’s published ABI, run arbitrary calls with only a deadline field, so the delay may not bind that path.

24.2M arcUSDG held, cross-exposure 80

DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE is held by three contracts: the timelock (getMinDelay 86,400s), ValidatorConsensus (threshold 2 of 3 validators, all bare EOAs) and CheckpointManager. The timelock’s proposer, executor and canceller roles are held by ValidatorConsensus, CheckpointManager and one further address, a 2-of-4 Safe that is also one of the three owners of the Arcus pToken 2-of-3 Safe, which is why crossExposureScore is 80. Arcus publishes ABIs only, so the claim that the validator quorum can route around the delay comes from the signature of executeGovernanceAction (arbitrary calls plus a deadline, no delay field), not from source, and that function has never run (executedGovernanceCount is 0).
