Why this score

every dApp above inherits this

Six core L1 contracts on Ethereum mainnet (Rollup, SequencerInbox, CoreProxyAdmin, DelayedInbox, Bridge, Outbox - not the L2 itself) share one authority chain. EXECUTOR_ROLE is held directly by a 7-of-8 Gnosis Safe named by Robinhood’s own governance docs: Robinhood (2 seats), BitGo, Chainlink Labs, Fireblocks, Offchain Labs, Paxos and Talos - though no public source maps a specific address to a specific institution. A second EXECUTOR_ROLE holder is a real, correctly-configured 7-day TimelockController. Batch 8 found and fixed a real bug here: this scorer had a hardcoded note from batch 4 claiming zero PROPOSER_ROLE holders, silently asserted as current fact on every push since - a live replay now shows a real holder (a 6-of-8 Safe), so the timelock does gate something today. But that same Safe also holds CANCELLER_ROLE - whoever can propose a change can also cancel any attempt to veto it, so there’s still no real separation of powers. This group's 14 root signers (7 direct Safe seats + 7 owners of one nested 3-of-7 seat) share none with any other tracked group (crossExposureScore: 100).
