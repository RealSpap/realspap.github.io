PumpSwap upgrade: a 3-of-4 vault, no time lock

On 2026-11-15, 17:20:20 UTC, the PumpSwap program on Solana mainnet-beta was upgraded by a Squads vault that needs 3 of 4 signers and has a time lock of 0 seconds. The new code had been on chain in a buffer for about 27 hours.

Its ProgramData account is 6naEzKeUuFh1Jeeu51NXQgr5qkXgXtc9WKNct4xynVJc on Solana mainnet-beta, and its last deployment is at slot 469811067, in the transaction 2uB6vN55cWe95KmCpk5FH7jkaFoXjLt3MLDyETiLfuQVJzcUt1FeES8W1NEwJufvyQMeaWZfVdB7R1C7RELp8uCS, at 17:20:20 UTC on 15 November 2026.

The upgrade authority of the program is 7gZufwwAo17y5kg8FMyJy2phgpvv9RSdzWtdXiWHjFr8 on Solana mainnet-beta. I computed vault 0 (bump 254) of the Squads v4 multisig 2yMoQqQrtbhq3nQ3wFoQQawWS65qcqUXcwHEYha4rshW on Solana mainnet-beta, and it is the same address. That multisig has a threshold of 3, a time lock of 0 seconds and 4 members: 4tbEe4AquJWyMh7f7yE5poFChhsuPbeGh7EHVQCvq2N8, 5sFJMw5dC59oKGKA9LgeYQCSmYS6rLeCegxMkcM2Pwby, 6W6qsDbocrEs3vjri5Di15bf9PLRYhfxekPk25MhVHWz and 7zc8kzBr5wbsQKxYYv9uCMgfcW5zjxLEopvmuf3ig6iw, all on Solana mainnet-beta.

A time lock of 0 seconds means that the multisig does not delay the execution of a transaction once it is approved.

The code was on chain before the upgrade. The upgrade of PumpSwap used the buffer account HBGEzR1n1ZKCN97KRSWQUSuBoM2CGXCZf2zpWCHbV6Fj on Solana mainnet-beta, named as the buffer in the upgrade instruction. A buffer account holds the code to be deployed, and anyone can read it from the chain. That buffer received its first transaction at 13:48:49 UTC on 14 November 2026. At 14:03:45 UTC on 14 November 2026 (slot 469354509) its authority passed from the account 92gWFZMpGhWzVq5tjBnJxF9VnFwE3ydB2idJp9z2D9Nr on Solana mainnet-beta to the vault 7gZufwwAo17y5kg8FMyJy2phgpvv9RSdzWtdXiWHjFr8 on Solana mainnet-beta. After that transaction, the only transaction that touched the buffer is the upgrade itself, 27 hours and 16 minutes later.

The same holds for the other program: its buffer EZML7ZaRf3ddxuFVzD58UAwJMbS23ALhRvRVRdG7jWCp on Solana mainnet-beta passed to the same vault at 14:04:03 UTC on 14 November 2026 (slot 469354597), and the upgrade of 6EF8rrecthR5Dkzon8Nwu78hRvfCKubJ14M5uBEwF6P on Solana mainnet-beta followed at 17:20:10 UTC on 15 November 2026. Both buffers no longer exist, because the upgrade closes them.

The multisig itself gives no minimum delay: its time lock is 0 seconds, so three of the four members could approve and execute an upgrade at once. The window of about 27 hours is what happened here, and nothing in the multisig requires it.

On Solana mainnet-beta, the same vault 7gZufwwAo17y5kg8FMyJy2phgpvv9RSdzWtdXiWHjFr8 upgraded another program, 6EF8rrecthR5Dkzon8Nwu78hRvfCKubJ14M5uBEwF6P, ten seconds earlier, at slot 469811025 (17:20:10 UTC on 15 November 2026), and again at slot 469055660 (20:12:28 UTC on 13 November 2026).

This page says nothing about why the program was upgraded, about what changed in its code, about who the four members are, about who chose the timing, or about the intentions of the team. It reports public accounts on dates and gives no score.
