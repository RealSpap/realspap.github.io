The 27 hours before the PumpSwap upgrade

I replayed the buffer of the PumpSwap upgrade of 15 November 2026 transaction by transaction: its code was fully written at 13:49:06 UTC on 14 November and ran at 17:20:20 UTC on 15 November, 27 hours 31 minutes later.

27 h 31 minthe PumpSwap code sat finished in a public buffer before it ran

read at 18:56Z on 25 November 2026. This page needs no script and no tool to read.

Correction of 23 November 2026. This page said that the buffer was fully written at 13:49:21 UTC on 14 November 2026 and had waited 27 hours 30 minutes 59 seconds. The transaction of 13:49:21 UTC is a transfer, not a write. The last write is at 13:49:06 UTC, in block 469350379, and the wait is 27 hours 31 minutes 14 seconds. The headline figure, 27 h 31 min, stands. The page also said that the buffer received 1,258 transactions that together wrote 1,270,696 bytes: 1,256 of them hold a write, one creates the buffer and one is the transfer. The bytes and their SHA-256 are unchanged. DAO Times raised the time on 21 November 2026, and I read the buffer again that day.

At 17:20:20 UTC on 15 November 2026, the program PumpSwap (pAMMBay6oceH9fJKBRHGP5D4bD4sWpmSwMn52FMfXEA on Solana mainnet-beta) was upgraded in slot 469811067. An upgrade on Solana copies new code from a buffer account into the program, and anyone can read a buffer account before the upgrade runs. This page replays the life of that buffer, block by block, with the commands to do the same.

Block 469350301, 13:48:49 UTC on 14 November 2026. The buffer HBGEzR1n1ZKCN97KRSWQUSuBoM2CGXCZf2zpWCHbV6Fj on Solana mainnet-beta receives its earliest transaction: the creation of the account and its set-up as a buffer, signed by the account 92gWFZMpGhWzVq5tjBnJxF9VnFwE3ydB2idJp9z2D9Nr on Solana mainnet-beta, which holds its authority.

Blocks 469350322 to 469350379, 13:48:53 UTC to 13:49:06 UTC on 14 November 2026. The buffer receives 1,256 transactions that each hold one write, and together they write 1,270,696 bytes of code. The last of them is in block 469350379, at 13:49:06 UTC. No later transaction holds a write.

Block 469350454, 13:49:21 UTC on 14 November 2026. A transaction signed by the account 4yWkD5KrH6knPLfBzaFG5oZuYGgDp4jQDBfgpVsG1hqh on Solana mainnet-beta sends 1 lamport to each of 19 accounts, one of which is the buffer. It writes nothing. That account is neither the authority of the buffer nor the vault, and the chain does not say why it sent the lamports.

Block 469354509, 14:03:45 UTC on 14 November 2026. The authority of the buffer passes from the account 92gWFZMpGhWzVq5tjBnJxF9VnFwE3ydB2idJp9z2D9Nr on Solana mainnet-beta to the account 7gZufwwAo17y5kg8FMyJy2phgpvv9RSdzWtdXiWHjFr8 on Solana mainnet-beta, the vault that later runs the upgrade. The transaction is 4pxAnPnuwD2rHZBz84WWCa7Kq8DrX3MskSmFyQTMNUnfSWMYP5sLD1fBxJJFzNYeVGGLDJ29o6spakWvtQbZe1SR.

After block 469350454 and before the upgrade, one transaction touches the buffer: that change of authority. The newest five transactions of the buffer, listed by command 1 below, are the upgrade, the change of authority, the transfer and two writes.

Block 469811067, 17:20:20 UTC on 15 November 2026. The vault 7gZufwwAo17y5kg8FMyJy2phgpvv9RSdzWtdXiWHjFr8 on Solana mainnet-beta runs the upgrade of the program from this buffer. The transaction is 2uB6vN55cWe95KmCpk5FH7jkaFoXjLt3MLDyETiLfuQVJzcUt1FeES8W1NEwJufvyQMeaWZfVdB7R1C7RELp8uCS.

The delays come from block times, which are estimates that validators attach to a block. From 13:49:06 UTC to 17:20:20 UTC the buffer was complete and waiting for 27 hours 31 minutes 14 seconds. The authority passed to the vault 14 minutes 39 seconds after the last write; from 14:03:45 UTC to 17:20:20 UTC, since the vault held its authority, 27 hours 16 minutes 35 seconds. The node api.mainnet-beta.solana.com and the node solana-mainnet.gateway.tatum.io return the same three block times.

The code that waited is the code that ran. I rebuilt the buffer from its 1,256 write transactions: 1,270,696 bytes, SHA-256 d2ed30147f5eb202da7094567a7e5aec35d471487eeb8a081278c519d6b04966. At 19:04Z on 17 November 2026 I read the code held by the ProgramData account 6naEzKeUuFh1Jeeu51NXQgr5qkXgXtc9WKNct4xynVJc on Solana mainnet-beta, deployed at slot 469811067: its leading 1,270,696 bytes had the same SHA-256, and the rest of the account was zeros. PumpSwap was upgraded again at 19:28:12 UTC on 20 November 2026, in slot 471854493, so that account now holds other code and the last line of command 8 differs from the one before it; the reading of 17 November is the record of the match.

This page says nothing about why the program was upgraded, what changed in its code, who the signers are, who chose the timing, or whether 27 hours is long or short. The time lock of the multisig behind the vault is 0 seconds, as the note on the PumpSwap upgrade shows: the multisig itself imposed no delay, so the hours above are what happened, and no rule required them.

Limit

A reading of public accounts and transactions on public nodes of Solana mainnet-beta between 13:26Z and 14:38Z on 16 November 2026, again at 19:04Z on 17 November 2026, and again at 18:56Z on 25 November 2026 (the list of the 1,260 transactions of the buffer, the four that hold no write, the block times, the transfer and the ProgramData account), each node answering with the genesis hash of that network. The write instructions come from the transactions read on 16 November and read again by command 8 on 21 November; a confirmed transaction does not change. The buffer account no longer exists, so its content is rebuilt from the transactions that wrote it. The list of its transactions comes from the node api.mainnet-beta.solana.com, which keeps this history; another public node returns recent blocks only. The second node, solana-mainnet.gateway.tatum.io, answers five requests per minute on its free plan, hence the pause in the third command. The last command sends about 1262 requests and took about 46 minutes on 21 November 2026. Since the upgrade of 20 November 2026 the code held by ProgramData is not the code of the buffer, and the last line of command 8 differs from the line before it.
