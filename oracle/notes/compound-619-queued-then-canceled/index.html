Compound 619: queued, then canceled by the Guardian Safe

I read the governance of Compound on Ethereum mainnet at the block before one transaction and at a recent block, on two public archive nodes. Proposal 619 went from Queued to Canceled; the Proposal Guardian, the Guardian expiry, the Timelock delay and the Timelock admin read the same on both sides.

The proposal was queued in transaction 0x14a7b5cf8fd873e6b22285b42f355c97cda6165d66a6a1f4f062305552d38fa5 on Ethereum mainnet (chain ID 1), in block 26312562 (block timestamp 1793447519, which is 31 October 2026), status success. The Timelock logged QueueTransaction for one action: a call to setProposalGuardian on the Governor with the Safe as the guardian and an expiry argument of 1793491200, which is 1 November 2026.

The proposal was canceled in transaction 0x790d887130ed420e90c6b9ae6bcb334e14a5c74d4a8abc7daba50c4f3f1a3ba0 on Ethereum mainnet (chain ID 1), in block 26313736 (block timestamp 1793461655, which is 31 October 2026, the same day), status success. The transaction went to the Safe and carries 5 approvals in a signature field of 325 bytes: 4 signatures and 1 pre-approved hash. The Safe called cancel for proposal 619 on the Governor. In that transaction the Governor logged ProposalCanceled and the Timelock logged CancelTransaction for the same queued action.

I read the authority state at block 26313735, the block before the cancel, and at block 26494344, which is the recent block of this reading, on two public archive nodes (drpc and blastapi) that gave the same answer for every call. Each row of the table is one of those calls. Between the two blocks, the state of Proposal 619 changed; the Proposal Guardian, the Guardian expiry, the Timelock delay and the Timelock admin read the same at both blocks.

The chain does not say why it was canceled.

Limit

This page states what two transactions and the state of three contracts show on Ethereum mainnet (chain ID 1) at two blocks. It says nothing about why the proposal was canceled, what the signers intend or what was decided off chain. The nodes are archive nodes: a node that does not answer gives an UNREAD, never an empty answer.
