Research / Chain files

Chain files

One page per chain: what the oracle tracks there, three findings that belong to that chain with the calls that replay them, and what is not read.

Base

13 targets · 3 findings

13 contracts on Base are tracked and scored by an oracle on Base Sepolia, among them Morpho Blue, Aerodrome and the Uniswap factories. Base is where one Safe signs for the largest tracked lending contract and where Uniswap is governed from Ethereum through a forwarder.

Ethereum L1

39 targets · 3 findings

39 contracts on Ethereum mainnet are tracked and scored by an oracle on Sepolia: the deepest set, with Aave, Compound, Morpho, Lido, the stablecoin issuers and Ethena. Here the interesting questions are who can move the prices a lending market reads, and which seat one committee holds on several chains.

Arbitrum

13 targets · 3 findings

13 contracts on Arbitrum One are tracked and scored by an oracle on Arbitrum Sepolia: GMX, Aave, Compound, Pendle, Fluid, Uniswap and the Arbitrum Security Council. Arbitrum is where an address with no code can be a governor, and where one of our own published scores was wrong and had to be corrected.

Hyperliquid

16 targets · 3 findings

16 targets on Hyperliquid are tracked and scored by an oracle on HyperEVM testnet: the HyperCore validator set, the HIP-3 perpetual dexes that anyone can deploy, the staking managers on HyperEVM and the legacy bridge. Here an authority is often a HyperCore identity that is also, or is not, an EVM contract.

Tempo

14 targets · 3 findings

14 targets on Tempo mainnet are tracked and scored by an oracle on Tempo Moderato, the testnet: the stablecoins of the chain (TIP-20 tokens), the Morpho vaults and Morpho Blue, and the validator registry. On Tempo a token's roles are held in a precompile, so the authority is read from role events and from the account that holds each role.

Zcash

6 targets · 3 findings

6 Zcash targets are scored and attested: the consensus-rule authority of Zcash itself, the funding streams, two custody vaults and a bridge. Zcash has no contract VM, so there is no oracle contract: the scores are committed in a transaction on Zcash Testnet, and the page says what that proves and what it does not.

Robinhood Chain

63 targets · 3 findings

63 targets on Robinhood Chain are tracked and scored by an oracle on the Robinhood Chain testnet: the longest tail of small protocols of any chain here, the tokenized stocks, Uniswap, Chainlink, LayerZero, Morpho and the rollup's own authority. Robinhood Chain is the chain where the oracle started, and every target was re-derived from zero in turn.

Solana

26 targets · 3 findings

26 programs on Solana mainnet are tracked and scored by a native Anchor program on Devnet, one score account per target. Solana has no contract VM to call: the authority of a program is its upgrade authority and what sits behind it, a Squads multisig, a Realms governance, or a cross-chain executor, and each of those is a different shape.
